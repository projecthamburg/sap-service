# Repeatable intake and plan-review audit

Protocol: `sap-journey-audit-v1`. The versioned scenario contract is `tests/fixtures/journey-contract.json`. This protocol is for audit, governance and visual fidelity; it records evidence and limitations rather than treating a green aggregate score as proof of correctness.

For every run, retain the source commit, deployed build ID, timestamp, browser/device, scenario inputs, expected outcome, actual outcome and links to private evidence. Keep failed attempts and their resolutions. A fixture, prompt, model or tolerance change requires a new run; do not overwrite the prior result. Real applicant information, credentials and signed private URLs must never enter the public repository or Actions artifacts.

The five client scenarios must each produce a unique, real PDF with their own scope, calculation attachment and visible breakdown; upload a plan/photo through the actual form; receive a private reference; book a consultation; and have only that synthetic booking removed through the supported admin UI. The three applicant scenarios cover a résumé, availability and receipt. Exercise at least two incomplete estimator cases and incomplete/error applicant paths. Routine deployment audits do not create bookings or send email; the owner separately authorized the labelled end-to-end tests.

The large-plan extension belongs to client 04. Verify all non-overlapping ownership cores cover the original page exactly once; padding preserves context across boundaries; ragged outer edges are included; and returned evidence retains tile-to-page coordinates. Reassembly must preserve source geometry. Distinct rooms with the same label must remain distinct; overlapping contradictory readings must remain unresolved. An unsupported resolution or partial batch must not be reported as a complete review.

The plan/input alignment extension belongs to client 05. Keep original printed text, units, normalized readings, source locations, entered values and room associations separately. Test missing/extra zeros, metric/imperial mistakes, ambiguous labels, a partial floor versus an entire building, and gross versus net cleanable area. Do not average contradictory readings or silently change price inputs. Record the user's correction or reason for retaining an exceptional value. A model's confidence does not replace review of the plan.

Useful evidence combines Blueprint's source impact, Domscan's declared runtime state and responsive styles, decoded assets, actual form interactions, independent artwork and computer-vision review of screenshots/PDF pages. Refresh source hashes after changes. The static graph does not prove execution; a screenshot does not prove delivery; spatial coverage does not prove complete OCR.
